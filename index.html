[ ABOUT ]

StratDots is a real-time strategy war game where you command armies across dynamic battlefields. Choose your side, deploy your forces, and dominate the enemy.

Built with LÖVE2D (Lua), StratDots delivers intense strategic combat with 2 built-in maps, a custom map maker, and challenging AI opponents.

[ FEATURES ]

Real-Time Combat

Command infantry, cavalry, and tanks in dynamic battles with intelligent AI opponents.

Custom Maps

2 built-in historical maps (Medieval 1444 & WW2 1941) plus a custom map maker to design your own battlefields.

Unit Formations

Deploy units in line, arrow, single file, or grouped formations for strategic positioning.

Air Support

Build airbases and deploy Su-25 ground attack aircraft for devastating bombing runs.

Defensive Structures

Construct walls, artillery emplacements, and fortifications to hold the line.

Texture Packs

Customize your experience with Advanced Pack and custom texture packs you can make!.

Difficulty Modes

Easy, Medium, and Hard AI difficulty. Plus Sandbox Mode for unlimited resources.

[ GAMEPLAY ]

TEASER TRAILER

UNIT TYPES

  • INFANTRY - Fast, cheap, and versatile foot soldiers (50 coins)
  • CAVALRY - Rapid mounted units for flanking maneuvers (100 coins)
  • TANKS - Heavy armored units with devastating firepower (200 coins)

CONTROLS

  • LEFT CLICK - Select units
  • SHIFT + CLICK - Multi-select units
  • DRAG - Box selection
  • MIDDLE MOUSE - Pan camera
  • SCROLL WHEEL - Zoom in/out
  • R - Recenter camera
  • SPACE - Pause/Resume battle
  • F3 - Toggle FPS counter
  • F11 - Toggle fullscreen
  • F12 - Screenshot mode

STRATEGY TIPS

  • Deploy units along the center line for automatic positioning
  • Capture points generate income - control the map to fund your army
  • Use formations to control unit facing and positioning
  • Artillery emplacements provide long-range fire support
  • Aircraft bombing runs can devastate enemy concentrations
  • Walls slow enemy advances and protect key positions
